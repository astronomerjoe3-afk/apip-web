# Curriculum Architecture

## Curriculum Promise

Cognispark should teach physics as a connected climb:

1. Foundation: make meaning visible
2. Core: connect concepts into school physics reasoning
3. Advanced: move into abstraction, modeling, and transfer

The curriculum should not feel like a list of topics. It should feel like a sequence of reasoning powers students acquire.

## Curriculum Pillars

| Pillar | Student Power | Example Topics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Measurement and representation | turn observations into quantities, graphs, and models | units, uncertainty, graph reading |
| Motion and change | read how systems change over time | kinematics, acceleration, graph meaning |
| Force systems | explain motion changes through interactions | resultant force, equilibrium, torque |
| Energy and transfer | track stores, pathways, losses, and rates | work, power, efficiency, thermal energy |
| Matter and fields | model invisible structure and interaction | particles, pressure, electric and magnetic fields |
| Waves and signals | reason across oscillation, propagation, and information | sound, light, optics, spectra |
| Atomic and nuclear | connect microscopic events to macroscopic evidence | radioactivity, quantum phenomena |
| Earth and cosmos | use physics to explain planetary and stellar systems | gravity, astrophysics, cosmology |

## Flagship Module: M1 Motion And Kinematics

M1 is the flagship because it attacks the most reusable early physics weakness: reading change correctly.

### Module Outcome

By the end of M1, a learner can move between story, graph, table, diagram, and equation without losing the physical meaning.

### Six-Mission Structure

| Mission | Concept | Misconceptions | Required Interaction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M1.1 Graphs are records, not pictures | graph shape records quantity over time | graph line is physical path, flat means nothing happens | story-to-graph and graph-to-story matcher |
| M1.2 Distance, displacement, speed, velocity | quantity choice changes meaning | distance equals displacement, speed equals velocity | route-map and signed-direction explorer |
| M1.3 Gradient means rate | slope has physical meaning | height equals speed, slope is just a trick | draggable slope interpreter |
| M1.4 Velocity-time area | area under velocity-time graph means displacement | area always means distance, negative velocity impossible | shaded-area and sign explorer |
| M1.5 Acceleration as velocity change | acceleration is change in velocity | acceleration only means speeding up, negative always means slowing | velocity-change timeline |
| M1.6 Constant-acceleration modeling | equations compress motion stories | formula hunting, all givens are equal | story to graph to equation capstone |

## Mission Anatomy

Every mission should include:

- hook: one concrete physical situation
- meaning setup: what the representation means before calculation
- misconception forecast: what students usually mix up
- live interaction: one control-based exploration
- guided checkpoint: one concept check with targeted correction
- worked example: understand mode and exam mode
- mastery lock: final transfer check
- review bridge: one future retrieval hook

## Lesson Types

| Lesson Type | Purpose | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Concept launch | introduce a new mental model | "A graph is a record, not a road." |
| Representation bridge | connect story, diagram, table, graph, and equation | distance-time story board |
| Misconception repair | explicitly correct a common wrong idea | flat graph segment does not mean time stopped |
| Exam transfer | apply concept under exam-style wording | interpolate on graph at a given time |
| Capstone mission | combine multiple representations and calculations | full rover journey reconstruction |

Phase 3:

- publish public standards pages for SEO and school procurement

## Curriculum Quality Bar

A module is flagship-ready only when:

- every lesson has a named concept target
- every checkpoint has a misconception mapping
- every simulation has an accessible non-visual explanation path
- every mission has at least one transfer item
- every module has a capstone task
- delayed review is triggered by actual weakness
