
# Cognispark Student Social Ad Pack

## Campaign Goal

Convince secondary-school students who feel stuck, bored, or overwhelmed by physics to try Cognispark as a more interactive way to learn.

## Target Audience

- Secondary-school physics students
- Students preparing for tests who are tired of passive note memorization
- Students who want clearer explanations, practice, and visible progress

## Core Promise

Cognispark helps students learn physics step by step through missions, visuals, simulations, checks, and instant feedback so they understand instead of cramming.

## Primary CTA

"Start your first mission at Cognispark."

## Recommended Format

- Aspect ratio: 9:16
- Duration: 35-40 seconds
- Platforms: Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels
- Style: fast, clean, bold, subtitle-first
- Editing rhythm: one main idea every 4-6 seconds
